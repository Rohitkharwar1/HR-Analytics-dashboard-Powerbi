HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI
Project Overview

This project focuses on building an HR Analytics Dashboard to analyze workforce trends, understand employee attrition patterns, and support data-driven decision-making for HR teams. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee attrition using interactive Power BI visualizations.

Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following visuals:

Stacked Bar Chart

Area Chart

Donut Chart

Table View

Treemap

Department and Gender Slicers

The objective is to identify key factors influencing employee attrition and retention.

Key Insights
Overall Attrition

A significant number of employees left the organization during the analyzed period. Certain departments show consistently higher attrition, highlighting the need for focused retention strategies.

Attrition by Age

The 25–35 age group records the highest attrition. Younger employees tend to switch jobs for better career growth, compensation, and work-life balance.

Attrition by Education Background

Employees with Bachelor’s degrees show the highest attrition, indicating strong market demand and increased job mobility for this group.

Attrition by Salary Slab

Employees in lower salary bands experience the highest attrition, emphasizing compensation as a critical driver of employee turnover.

Attrition by Job Role

Roles such as Sales Executives, Laboratory Technicians, and HR Executives show higher attrition, possibly due to workload, performance pressure, and role expectations.

Job Satisfaction Analysis

Employees with low job satisfaction scores exhibit significantly higher attrition, demonstrating the strong relationship between satisfaction and retention.

Department and Gender Analysis

Department slicers allow quick comparison across Sales, R&D, and HR. Treemap analysis indicates slightly higher attrition among male employees, varying by department.
